About
Zubeen Garg

Zubeen Garg was one of Assam's most beloved and distinctive musical voices — a singer, composer, lyricist, actor and filmmaker whose work reached far beyond one language or one generation.

From Assamese melodies to Hindi and other Indian languages, his voice carried an unmistakable warmth, intensity and emotional depth. His songs became part of everyday life for countless listeners, while his contribution to Assamese music and culture made him a truly iconic figure.

This space is a small tribute to that extraordinary musical journey — a place to pause, listen and remember the voice that gave so many moments their meaning.
